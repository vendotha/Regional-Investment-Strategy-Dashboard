# 📊 Regional Investment Strategy Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Regional Investment Strategy Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze regional sales performance and support data-driven investment decisions. It provides insights into revenue, profitability, customer orders, and product category performance using dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Businesses operating across multiple regions need a centralized view of sales performance to identify profitable markets and optimize investment decisions. This dashboard helps decision-makers compare regional performance, monitor profitability, analyze product categories, and identify the best regions for future investments.

## 📈 Key Insights

✔ Southwest region generated the highest revenue and is recommended for future investment.

✔ Canada and Northwest also demonstrated strong business performance.

✔ Bikes contributed the highest share of overall revenue.

✔ The company generated approximately **77.5M** in revenue.

✔ Overall profit is approximately **999.5K**.

✔ Profit Margin is **1.3%**, indicating opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

✔ Interactive slicers allow users to analyze performance by Year, Region, Category, and Business Type.
